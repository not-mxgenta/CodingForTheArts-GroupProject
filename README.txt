OVERVIEW OF PROJECT

STALK is a suspenseful point-and-click game that follows Quinn, a night-shift worker heading home after a long shift.
On the walk back, Quinn senses something unsettling—like they're being watched. Dismissing the feeling, they make it home safely... or so it seems.
As night falls, players must guide Quinn through routine tasks around the house while staying alert. A dangerous escaped patient from a nearby mental hospital is lurking in the shadows,
and survival depends on your awareness and choices.


Features

Branching Narrative: Make critical decisions when prompted—each choice directly influences the story's direction and outcome.
Multiple Endings: Experience a variety of possible endings, both good and bad, based on your actions and awareness.
Replayability: After each ending or death, restart the game and explore different narrative paths by making new choices.
Tension-Driven Gameplay: Stay alert and manage everyday tasks while sensing an ever-present threat nearby.


How to Play

Movement: Use the WASD keys to move during exploration sections outside the house.
Point-and-Click Navigation: Inside the house, interact with your environment by pointing and clicking on objects and areas of interest.
Complete Tasks: Progress by completing various household challenges and objectives.
Make Choices: Choose dialogue options carefully—your decisions shape the story and determine which ending you receive.
Use the Mini Map: A mini map is available to help you navigate the house and keep track of important locations.


Development Details

STALK was developed using Visual Studio Code with the p5.js JavaScript library. 
The game combines both keyboard and mouse interactions to create a hybrid experience of exploration and decision-making.
p5.js was used to handle rendering, interaction, and game logic.

Credits

Sound Effects & Music: Sourced from Pixabay.

Graphics: Created using Aseprite and Pixilart.

To enable saving of player data across sessions we used node.js.
